1Sky viewer: three layouts, plus landing, blink set-up and states

What works. Every artboard shares one player: play, step, scrub, speed, Blink, A/B picking, crosshair, path arrow and the coordinate field are live. Drag any image area to tilt it in 3D; drag the globe to spin it.

What is placeholder. The product name, all sky images, dates, counts and example coordinates. Images are generated star fields with one dot that drifts. Nothing here is real mission data.

Decisions I made for you. The globe lives on the landing page and leads into the tilting image. Only the Blink and Data tabs have content. All five states are mocked. Four switches are visual only (marked in the panel).
