See how any patch of sky changes over time

Space telescopes photograph the same places again and again. This tool lines those photographs up so anyone can play them back and spot what moved or changed.

1. Pick a spot

Spin the globe, search a name or type coordinates.

2. Play the images

Every image of that spot, in date order, like a short film.

3. Blink two of them

Flip between two dates. Stars stay put. Movers jump.

Three promises

We call things candidates, never discoveries.

Every number shown is fetched or measured. Anything else is labelled as a placeholder.

Looking never needs an account.
